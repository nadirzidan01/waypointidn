How to Order

Simple & Easy Guide to Order Supplies Directly to Your Vessel

1

Select Products

Browse through categories and click + Add to Cart for items you wish to buy.

2

Fill In Details

Scroll to cart section, enter your full Crew Name and Vessel Name accurately.

3

Choose Payment

Select your preferred method: COD, QRIS, or Bank Transfer (BCA/Mandiri).

4

Confirm & Send (WA)

Click Proceed and send your order via WhatsApp. For Transfer/QRIS, attach your payment receipt photo!
